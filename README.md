# Mudhop

Source for [mudhop.com](https://mudhop.com/).

## Artwork

The Mudhop mascot is based on “P.E. Mudhops,” a historical cartoon signed by Walt Dunning. It appears alongside Dave Newman’s “Yardclerk with a Capital J, or, Woes of a Mudhop” on page 19 of _Pacific Electric Magazine_, April 1944, Vol. 24, No. 10, published by Pacific Electric Railway Company. [View the archived issue][source].
